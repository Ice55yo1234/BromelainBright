About Bromelain Bright

Redefining beauty with science and sustainability.

The Brand Story

Bromelain Bright started as a university research project with a bold question: Can agricultural waste become a luxury skincare ingredient?

Thailand produces over 2 million tons of pineapple annually, and a significant portion — the cores — is discarded. Our team discovered that these cores are rich in Bromelain, a remarkable enzyme that gently exfoliates, brightens, and rejuvenates skin.

Today, Bromelain Bright transforms this waste into premium skincare products. We work directly with Thai farming communities, creating economic value from what was once considered garbage. Every product tells a story of innovation, sustainability, and care.

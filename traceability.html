Transparency

Trace Your Product

Follow the journey from farm to your doorstep. Full transparency, every step.

🌾

Farm

Rayong Province, Thailand

Pineapple cores collected from our partner farms in Rayong — Thailand's premium pineapple region. Farmers receive fair compensation for waste that would otherwise be discarded.

🚛

Collection

Within 24 hours of harvesting

Fresh pineapple cores are collected within 24 hours to ensure maximum Bromelain potency. Temperature-controlled transport maintains enzyme integrity.

⚗️

Processing

Cold-extraction facility

Cores undergo our proprietary cold-extraction process to isolate Bromelain enzyme. No heat, no harsh chemicals — preserving nature's power in its purest form.

🔬

Laboratory

GMP Certified Lab

Every batch undergoes rigorous quality testing for potency, purity, and safety. Dermatological testing ensures suitability for all skin types.

📦

Packaging

Eco-friendly materials

Products are packaged in recyclable, eco-friendly materials. Glass jars are reusable, sachets are biodegradable, and all printed with soy-based inks.

✨

Customer

You!

Your Bromelain Bright product arrives with a unique QR code linking back to this journey. You know exactly where your skincare comes from.

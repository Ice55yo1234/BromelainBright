Our Story

Bromelain Bright

Where science meets sustainability, and innovation meets nature.

Our Beginning

Bromelain Bright was born from a simple observation: Thailand's pineapple industry discards millions of kilograms of cores every year. We asked — what if this waste could become something beautiful?

Our founders, a team of biochemists and skincare enthusiasts from Khon Kaen University, Nong Khai Campus, discovered that pineapple cores contain exceptionally high concentrations of Bromelain — a powerful enzyme with remarkable skincare properties.

🎯

Our Mission

To create premium skincare products from upcycled agricultural waste, proving that sustainability and luxury can coexist beautifully.

🌟

Our Vision

To become the global leader in enzyme-based sustainable skincare, transforming waste streams into beauty innovations worldwide.

Milestones

Our Journey

2022 — Research Begins

Initial research on Bromelain enzyme extraction from pineapple cores at Khon Kaen University, Nong Khai Campus.

2023 — First Prototype

Successfully developed the first Bromelain-based facial mask formula. Clinical trials begin.

2023 — ISKKU Competition

Presented at ISKKU x HEX startup competition, receiving recognition for innovation.

2024 — Official Launch

Launched three flagship products: Sachet, Jar, and Essence. Partnership with 15+ pineapple farms.

2024 — Growing Impact

5,000+ customers, 2,500kg of pineapple waste upcycled, and expanding into international markets.

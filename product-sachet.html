Scientific Background

Bromelain is a group of proteolytic enzymes found in pineapple (Ananas comosus). Our extraction process from pineapple cores — normally discarded as agricultural waste — preserves maximum enzymatic activity for gentle yet effective skin exfoliation and brightening.

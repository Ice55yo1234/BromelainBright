The Enzyme

What is Bromelain?

Bromelain is a complex mixture of proteolytic enzymes derived from the pineapple plant (Ananas comosus). Found primarily in the stem and core — parts typically discarded during processing — Bromelain has been extensively studied for its therapeutic properties.

Our proprietary extraction process preserves the enzymatic activity at its peak, ensuring maximum efficacy in every product.
